# Estasy Project

## What is this?
This project is like a big toy shop on the computer. People can look at clothes, shoes, and other things, put them in a cart, and buy them.

## Who helps make it?
- `client/` is the part you can see and touch. It is like the shop windows and buttons.
- `server/` is the part that thinks and remembers. It is like the shopkeeper and the storage room.

## What does the shop do?
- Shows products to people.
- Lets people log in and make an account.
- Lets people add items to their cart.
- Lets people finish buying things.
- Keeps orders safe.
- Lets people write reviews.

## What are the important pieces?
- `client/src/app/` has the pages people see.
- `client/src/components/` has the pieces like product cards and menus.
- `server/src/controllers/` has the helpers that decide what the shop does.
- `server/src/models/` has the toy box rules for products, users, and orders.
- `server/src/routes/` tells the shop what to do when someone clicks.

## Why is it special?
This project is special because it is a whole shopping world to play with, built with two parts:
- One part for the people who visit the shop.
- One part for the shop to keep things working.

## How do you use it?
1. Open the shop on your computer.
2. Look at the products.
3. Add things to the cart.
4. Buy them.

## In short
This project is a friendly online shop for clothes and other things. It helps people look, choose, and buy, just like a real store, but on the computer.
